Vault Raiders

Mission Briefing & Rules

🎯 The Heist Goal

Race your rival thieves into the vault. Snatch the Master Diamond ($1,000) or Gold Ingots ($300), and carry them to the 🚐 Getaway Van at the top to bank your cash!

👥 Recommended Crew Size

Best with 3 or 4+ players! 1 or 2 players is good to practice, but 3 or 4 players unleashes hilarious tackle fights, banana slips, and rocket chases!

🕹️ Controls

Move Thief: WASD / Arrows
Sprint / Tackle: Spacebar
Use Power-Up: E or F
On Mobile: Touch Joystick

📦 Mystery Power-Ups

🍌
Banana Peel

Drop behind you. Rivals slip, spin 360°, and drop their loot!

🚀
Rocket Boost

3x supersonic speed! Ram opponents into walls to stun them.

🧲
Loot Magnet

Pulls the diamond directly into your hands from afar.

💨
Smoke Bomb

Blinds security guard drones so you can sneak past safely.

⚠️ Security Threat

Guards patrol with sweeping flashlight cones. If you touch the light: SIRENS WAIL! If the guard catches you, you drop all your loot and get sent back to spawn!
